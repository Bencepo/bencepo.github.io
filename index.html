The collection

Five addons, one style

All of them are free, all of them work together, and none of them needs a configuration marathon.

LevelTrail

Your leveling run, analysed

Records every level, death, XP source, gold day and dungeon visit. Paste the export into the page and see your run split by split, with records and a share image.

v1.3.6-beta
Open the pageCurseForgeWago

VendorFloor

Auction house sniper

Finds auctions below vendor price, below disenchant value and profitable crafts. One click to buy, collect and sell. The page shows your real profit per day and item.

v0.8.6
Open the pageCurseForgeWago

ProcFlare

Never miss a proc

A big pulsing Clearcasting alert with sound and screen flash, and small icons with a countdown for weapon, trinket and talent procs. Nothing to set up.

v0.2.0
Details & demoCurseForgeWago

SwingCast

Cast bar plus swing timer

Adds spell icon, cast time, latency zone and a swing timer to the standard cast bar. The strip lights up while Maul, Heroic Strike or Cleave is queued.

v0.4.4
Details & demoCurseForgeWago

StatTrim

A tidy character window

Hide, merge and reorder categories and stats right inside the character window. Edit with checkboxes and arrows, works in combat.

Import hub

Paste any export

LevelTrail (/trail) or VendorFloor (Menu > Export): paste it here and the right page opens with your data. Nothing is uploaded, the page reads it in your browser.

  • LevelTrail: time per level, XP sources, deaths, gear, records and a share image.
  • VendorFloor: profit per day, best items, time to sale, cumulative profit and a share image.

How they are built

Small on purpose

✦
Buttons, not commandsEvery setting lives in the options window or behind the minimap button. Chat commands exist, but you never need them.
◈
Light and quietNo work while nothing happens. Each release gets a performance review and a code check with the WoW API annotations.
⛉
Private by designThe web pages read your export in the browser. No accounts, no uploads, no tracking.
⚒
Made for ForeverBuilt around Forever's limits, such as the hidden combat log and secret values, instead of pretending they are not there.
