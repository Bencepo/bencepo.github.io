Addons for WoW Forever

Two small addons that record what you do, and two pages that show it. Free, private, nothing is uploaded.

LevelTrail

Your leveling run split by split: time per level, XP sources, deaths, gold, library books, alts and a share image.

Open LevelTrail →

VendorFloor

Buys auctions listed below vendor price, collects them from the mail and sells them. The page shows profit per day and your best items.
