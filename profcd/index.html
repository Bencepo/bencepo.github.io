Profession cooldowns at a glance · v1.0.1

ProfCD

Hover the minimap button for the transmute, Mooncloth and Salt Shaker timers of all your characters. Click it to see which crafts you have the mats for.

How it plays

  • Hover the minimap button: every cooldown of every character, with the time left.
  • Click it: the window lists each craft with its mats, in bags, in the bank or missing.
  • Every transmute has its own cooldown in Forever, so each gets its own row.
  • Alts show as of their last login.
  • No minimap button? ProfCD makes a macro: one click opens the window, the next closes it.
Features

What it does

◷
Timers on hoverHover the minimap button: every tracked cooldown of every character, with the time left or "Ready".
⚗
Mats checkClick it: each craft shows "Mats in bags", "Mats in bank" or what is missing, and how many crafts the mats are enough for.
⧗
Forever cooldownsEvery transmute on its own cooldown (Arcanite, Truesilver, Gold, the elemental ones), plus Mooncloth and the Salt Shaker. Durations come from the game.
☰
Recipes from the gameReagents are read from the profession window, so recipe changes in Forever show up on their own.
◎
Alts includedOther characters show as of their last login. /pcd forget removes one.
⌘
Macro instead of a buttonTurn the minimap button off and ProfCD creates a macro: one click opens the window, the next closes it.

Settings

  • ◆Its own window: the minimap button, /pcd or the addon menu at the minimap. Drag it anywhere, it stays open while you play.
  • ◆Minimap button, Show other characters, Chat notice when ready.
  • ◆/pcd minimap toggles the button, /pcd forget <name> removes a character.

Changelog

1.0.1
  • New icon in the addon list and on the minimap button, part of the new Bencepo logo family.
1.0.0
  • Minimap button with a tooltip: every tracked profession cooldown of all your characters, with the time left.
  • Window with the mats status of each craft: in bags, in bank, or what is missing.
  • Tracks every transmute on its own cooldown (Arcanite, Truesilver, Gold, elemental transmutes), Mooncloth and the Salt Shaker.
  • Reagents are read from the profession window, so recipe changes in Forever are picked up.
  • Macro "ProfCD" is created when the minimap button is turned off.
  • Chat notice when a cooldown is ready. /pcd forget <name> removes a character.
  • Settings in the window, with a "Report a bug" button.
